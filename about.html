📖 Our Story Background

Rethabile's Flame Burger started as a humble local weekend street food stall in 2024. Fueled by a signature hardwood charcoal technique and a secret baseline sweet-heat basting formula, we expanded into a permanent flagship restaurant in 2025.

🌟 Our Core Values

  • Uncompromised Quality: 100% local South African beef smashed live on premium seasoned flat-tops.
  • Farm Fresh Sourcing: Partnering directly with regional farm blocks to bring the best ingredients to your plate daily.
