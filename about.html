    The flame Grill Heritage

    Rethabile's Flame Burger started as a humble pop-up grill block in 2024. Fueled by a customer hardwood charcoal technique and a secret baseline sweet-heat formula, we have expanded into a modern, high energy joint hubs across Gauteng.

    Our Core Values

    Uncompromised Quality: 100% grass-fed local South African beef smashed live on premium seasoned flat-tops.

    Retail Sourcing: Bottling and parking our signature spice blends to let clients take the flame-grill profile home.
